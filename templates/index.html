DESMODROMIC SYSTEM

ENGINEERED FOR THE TRACK

Unlike standard engines, our Desmodromic valve control system mechanically closes the valves with high precision at high RPMs, allowing up to 14,500 RPM on street-legal engines.

  • Counter-rotating crankshaft to counteract wheel inertia
  • Twin Pulse firing order for predictable power delivery
  • Bosch 6-axis Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) electronics package

A CENTURY ON DISPLAY IN BOLOGNA

One hundred years ago, in a city that was learning to imagine the future, three brothers sparked an idea. From that moment on, Bologna and Ducati have never parted ways: the same road, travelled together for a century.

From 4 September to 5 October 2026, we brought that story back to the very places where it was born and grew, creating a city-wide exhibition across Bologna. An invitation to rediscover a shared heritage: the story of a brand that has carried Bologna and Italy to every corner of the world.

Four iconic locations in the historic city centre hosted four chapters of a century-long story that continues to belong to the entire city.
